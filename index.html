About

Research statement, skills and references

A crease pattern that folds cleanly on paper stops working once the panels have real thickness: the facets collide, and the single degree of freedom that made the pattern useful disappears. My work is about restoring that motion in thick, load-bearing panels — and about getting more than one folded configuration out of a single assembly.

At POSTECH I proposed an extended offset crease method that accommodates thickness while keeping a degree-6 vertex foldable in two directions, and analyzed the resulting kinematics with a Lagrangian multiplier formulation. I also build what I design: laser-cut panel prototypes, pneumatic actuators based on thickness-accommodated Kresling patterns, and finite element models for industrial structures.
